# Cooling interventions: research notes (1 Oct 2026)

Web research for extra cooling options and their costs. Costs are **not in the app yet** (deferred);
they are recorded here for the report and a possible later cost feature.
Conversions at 51.5 EGP per USD (rate on 29 Sep 2026).

## Options

| Option | Effect (published) | Cost per m² | In app? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cool roofs | Model: −4.64 °C (dark) / −3.60 °C (pale) per unit area coated; Wang, Huang & Li 2020 (GRL) sensitivity, ACPA albedo table | Coatings $1.6–27/m² (≈ EGP 80–1,400), US prices; no Egyptian reflective-paint price found (Egyptian foam insulation EGP 200–450/m² is a different product) | Yes |
| Street trees | Model (our regression): vegetation vs dark ground | Egypt 100 Million Trees programme ≈ EGP 26–30 per sapling planted (planting only); established street trees $150–1,500 internationally | Yes |
| Cool pavements | UAE desert field study: albedo 0.24 → 0.70 cut surface 60 → 47 °C (up to −15 °C). Cairo study: 0.12 → 0.50 up to −12.9 °C at peak. Model uses −2.24 °C per unit area (conservative) | $1.70–37.75/m², typical ≈ $7/m² (≈ EGP 360) | Yes (1 Oct) |
| Pocket parks on sandy lots | Vacant lots raise LST; greening cools (US, Tehran studies). Model (our regression): −6.5 °C per unit area | Natural grass EGP 90–160/m² installed; native-plant landscaping EGP 250–400/m²; xeriscape $54–215/m² (US), 50–75% less water | Yes (1 Oct) |
| Green roofs | Up to ~3 °C cooling in dry climates; far cooler roof surfaces | $107–215/m² (≈ EGP 5,500–11,000), needs irrigation and structural capacity | No (report only) |
| Shade sails | Ground under sail up to −16 °C; pedestrian PET −6 to −10 °C | not researched | No: satellites see the sail's hot top surface |
